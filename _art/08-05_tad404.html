So! Yeah!! I wanted there to be a follow-up to [the TADC doodle](movie-day) before I even watched the show, but I wasn't compelled enough by the end to make anything. Fast-forward a few weeks and these ideas spawned. Comics take a long time (these were about 9 hours combined, both on the same canvas) but I miss doing them. I still have all the assets and palettes from [the actual project](https://a-flyleaf.github.io/hello-world); give or take some brush settings, they still look the same. Thought I might have trouble keeping the cast consistent after so long, but they've been doodled here and there over the years so (IMO) they don't look too off-model at all! Neat. Used a smidge of Blender for the house of cards (traced over it) and the panel with the knockoff-Caine army (array modifier my beloved). Here is a singular knockoff-Caine-clone, in all his pseudo-MSPaint glory. Feel free to commit atrocities with him.
