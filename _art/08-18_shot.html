If I had a nickel for every time I've done a [screenshot](https://youtu.be/L1G1V7nwgCk?si=Zc36tpXlE26ueLEw&t=64) study of this character looking unhinged while aiming a gun, I'd have two nickels. Which is actually a very small amount of nickels, all considered.
This was meant to be quick, messy painting practice and then I got lost in the blending sauce. Colors eyeballed from a sightly brightened edit; merged the sketch down and rendered mostly on one layer, save the stray adjustment & highlight. Time taken not noted; started about 4 hours from finish, but there were several breaks between.
Character who made me understand what people mean when they're like "this one's my favorite, he sucks and I hate him." Diversity winloss for me personally. I have nothing else to say.
