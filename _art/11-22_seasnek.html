Doodle that I felt like upscaling and painting over. Was browsing the work of [Holly Lucero](https://linktr.ee/sparrowlucero), whose [Flight Rising art](https://sparrowlucero.tumblr.com/tagged/flight%20rising) reminded me that canon designs are fake and I can do whatever I want forever. I have a go-to scrungly-creature face shape and it shows.
Time taken: about 2 hours. Features a [serpentine dragon](https://www1.flightrising.com/dragon/57362941) who does not have horns, but who *used* to have horns, and so some former-feature liberties were taken. I could definitely refine it some, but I didn't plan anything & am satifsied enough with the quick-ish exercise it was meant to be!
