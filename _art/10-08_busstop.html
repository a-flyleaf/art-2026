\*"a-feuc" = a-flyleaf expanded universe clusterfuck, AKA what happens when I get possessed with the urge to draw all my human characters in one go and also in the same scene. But limited to main characters only, for my sanity. I don't want to know how long it took!!!!1!
Decided to torture/challenge myself, while I was at it, to put everyone in different clothing from Cartoon Character Default. There are not *huge* changes for most of them, granted. My only outfit design principles are "does the distance/no-detail version read cleanly" and "would I feel like drawing it a billion times in a row (e.g. for animation or comicking)," so it always skews simple.
I wanted to try something different with the lines this time: blocking in the darkest areas wholesale, *then* filling the rest. It would probably have been more noticeably time-saving if I wasn't doing it for 8 fullbodies in a row.
Didn't know what to stick in the background, didn't feel like scribbling in detail; roughly smushed together [these](https://www.pexels.com/photo/building-facade-with-shops-and-sidewalk-in-city-in-winter-6588648/) [three](https://www.pexels.com/photo/photo-of-store-front-3988037/) [photos](https://www.pexels.com/photo/book-shop-facade-against-modern-car-in-winter-city-6390200/), tossed in a couple trees to fill side space, scribbled in a bunch of overlays *bam* we done here.
