\*"a-feuc" = a-flyleaf expanded universe clusterfuck, AKA what happens when I get possessed with the urge to draw all my human characters in one go and also in the same scene. Limited to main character(s) only, for my sanity. I don't want to know how long it took!!!!1!
[IMG: initial sketch, blender, palette, palette iPad] Figured I'd model the actual structure so I wouldn't have to figure out perspective. Then actually designed the thing, *re-*modeled it, and went ham on tiny little details that are literally not noticeable at all in the final thing.
Decided to torture/challenge myself, while I was at it, to put everyone in different clothing from Cartoon Character Default. There are not *huge* changes for most of them, granted. My only outfit design principles are "does the distance/no-detail version read cleanly" and "would I feel like drawing it a billion times in a row (e.g. for animation or comicking)," so it always skews simple.
[IMG: heights] Ignore the barbie doll anatomy. I had the passing thought of modifying that base stick-figure to be proportionate for everyone, then decided *that would take too much got damn time*, threw the PNG in the 3D scene, and eyeballed it. [IMG: blender file]
I wanted to try something different with the lines this time: blocking in the darkest areas wholesale, *then* filling the rest. It would probably have been more noticeably time-saving if I wasn't doing it for 8 fullbodies in a row.
[IMG: lines; lines+base colors (gradients included); line colors no overlays] I wasn't strictly planning on coloring the lines. Then I thought the contrast looked all out of whack with dark everywhere. Did I plan for this in advance? [Of course not.](https://64.media.tumblr.com/e03c295296b72c8a3433c88bf6a6889c/3b9234719fa02838-0e/s1280x1920/26a62627fa1145c34807717b4e5b5504bc5f7a08.png)
Didn't know what to stick in the background, didn't feel like scribbling in detail; roughly smushed together [these](https://www.pexels.com/photo/building-facade-with-shops-and-sidewalk-in-city-in-winter-6588648/) [three](https://www.pexels.com/photo/photo-of-store-front-3988037/) [photos](https://www.pexels.com/photo/book-shop-facade-against-modern-car-in-winter-city-6390200/), tossed in a couple trees to fill side space, scribbled in a bunch of overlays *bam* we done here.
[IMG: layer-adjusted version?] Part of the overlay spam was a minor levels adjustment. I kinda like this version more :V but it's a *bit* too vibrant for the initially-intended mood.
