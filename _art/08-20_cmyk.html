Lost internet, considered finally fixing the [Toyshelf](https://a-flyleaf.github.io/toyshelf) tagging system, went *nah* and whipped up some new "thumbnails" for these losers instead. Some of their designs are more consistent than others.
random relevant factoids: - You know those digital artist memes about having unsearchable keysmash filenames? I did that at first. Then I got sick of it. In 2014, I started keeping random doodle-sketches in files titled by date. This does not solve any "where the fuck did I put that one scribble" problems, but it looks nice.  Anyway, the default canvas size was 500×500px for years---including through 2018, when most of these characters were developed. Hence the size of these! - I try to avoid coloring with base colors + overlay spam. (There's nothing Inherently Wrong with it, it just tends to feel cheap/lazy to me. Uniting a color scheme from the getgo tends to be faster, as a plus.) These were all colored with base defaults and overlay spam anyway, because I did not have one singular iota of a palette plan going in.
time taken: about 7 hours, breaks not included, plus some untimed handful of minutes for next-day fixes. Also not including code fuckery on this webpage---primarily for the finished art, but also for this description. It's different if your browser's wide or thin!
