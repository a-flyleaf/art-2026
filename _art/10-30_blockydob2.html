> be me > design & model a random character on a whim > block everything out wicked fast > set it aside for a while because the design looks off > still want to futz with 3D in the interim > "hey the hands were easy this time. what if I give that dog more than 2 pointers and a thumb" > *proceed to spend hundreds of hours touching up basically everything everywhere* A neat little glow-up, if I do say so myself!
patch notes & resources used
- Small boatload of "**backend**"/invisible updates: - [cleared leftover keyframes](https://www.reddit.com/r/blender/comments/1bklfzt/how_do_you_delete_all_keyframes/), because I do everything in One Gigantic Megafile. idk how people properly manage/separate assets yet - various outfit & masking toggles - lots of vertex cleanup & tweakage (unused groups & odd weights) - unwieldy base texture at least now has a node frame - made offshoot/solid textures for most objects & part of the base - unfucked the mouth fusion, which is a thing that happened, somehow, somewhere along the line?? - also unfucked various bones, independent of weight paints - things I watched, ended up not using, but still think are useful: - YouTube: [Hard Surface Modeling Tips](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jD0t6kcq7zM) + [6 Hard-Surface Modeling Tricks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ml2t8uxPAQU) - YT: [How to Bake Normal Maps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dPbrhqqrZck) - YT: [Baking Normals](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j3lwBDtC0ic) - YT: [Normal Maps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WJZTI3SSVBU) (no baking in this one!) - StackExchange: [rigging buttons](https://blender.stackexchange.com/questions/87357/how-to-rig-a-shirt-with-buttons-so-that-the-buttons-follow-the-mesh-of-the-shirt) - YT: [Boolean Modifier](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CHqH5oz0DvQ) - on making the **rig** more usable: - YouTube: [Fixing Rigify's broken fingers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlshSq4IX38) - StackOverflow: [linking constraints](https://blender.stackexchange.com/questions/178227/customizing-the-rigify-meta-rig-constraints-custom-drivers-whats-the-soluti) (only worked for me with `basic.raw_copy` bones) - StackExchange: [pinning custom properties](https://blender.stackexchange.com/questions/294897/can-i-change-blender-to-always-show-certain-panels-or-menus) (or any other panel!) - **Eyes** got redone again lol. I don't have to re-draw them every time I want to change the expression now!! Three layers: a boolean cutout object, interior sclera, and iris objects shrinkwrapped to the sclera. It still clips around the inner corner, but that can be either drawn over (see below) or fudged with shapekeys (as animated above). - **Modeled** a bunch of details that were previously flat or nonexistent: the diamond not-buttons, shoulder things, collar trim, black segments & back details on the top, and a proper belt. Still vastly simplified from [the original character design](https://www.artstation.com/artwork/XJW1n3), but I like this much better! - The pixel art on the previous versions were more a byproduct of the tutorial I was following than an intentional design choice. Some pixelization lingers, but having the most prominent details modeled out nice and Smoothe™ is much, *much* closer to the original vision. - also I don't like UVs and the wrangling thereof :V Feels like doing everything twice for, at least in this style, negligible benefit. - **Muzzle** is also more dynamic, with fully vector-based markings and an upper lip that can stretch and snarl; examples of that below. - The marking-smoothness was inspired by Noggi's dissections of the Guilty Gear style; I don't remember in which of the [two](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tWcaQ3gCbUU) [videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=234m7y8D3cE) it was covered, but the game gets extremely crisp color edges by simply drawing 3D edges in the same places. On a flat surface I think you'd normally accomplish that with a highres texture. But, again, I don't like UV-wrangling!! We can break the edge flow a little. As a treat. - **Shoes** did not get re-modeled but they're shiny now. - YT: [Three-Point Lighting Setup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sN8hS2vLG4o), used for the turnaround above. HDRIs here & below are all Blender defaults. - I linked a video on light linking [before](japhet) but missed a step this round.
