> be me > design & model a random character on a whim > block everything out wicked fast > set it aside for a while because the design looks off > still want to futz with 3D in the interim > "hey the hands were easy this time. what if I give that dog more than 2 pointers and a thumb" > *proceed to spend hundreds of hours touching up basically everything everywhere* A neat little glow-up, if I do say so myself!
patch notes & resources used
- Small boatload of "backend"/invisible changes: - - The pixel art details in the previous version were more a byproduct of the tutorial than an intentional part of the design. Having (nearly\*) everything modeled out nice and Smoothe™ is much, *much* closer to the original vision. - \*Nearly, not *everything-*everything. The base outfit is set up with multiple layers of UV fuckery, which I did not feel like redoing from effectively-scratch. - I don't have to redraw the eyes every time I want to change the expression!! They were still a hassle given the unconventional shape, but it's easier to tweak vertices with shapekeys than redo every single pixel each time!!!
