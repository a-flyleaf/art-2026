Saw [this image](https://kosmogrl.tumblr.com/post/826604045555859456) a while back, immediately thought of the [brame animal.](https://a-flyleaf.github.io/toyshelf/misc#brame) Started redrawing it, then had the idea to dust off that model from a while back. Ta-da~ The original image is AI-generated and/or crudely photoshopped, but in the process of trying to source it anyway I found multiple incidents of a deer actually getting hit by and flying *into* a bus. [Wild.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FxHEwr-yXDs) No animals were harmed in the making of this image, meanwhile, although I *did* cheat perspective with reckless abandon.
