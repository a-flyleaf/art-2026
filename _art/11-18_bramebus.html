Saw [this image](https://kosmogrl.tumblr.com/post/826604045555859456) a while back, immediately thought of the [brame animal.](https://a-flyleaf.github.io/toyshelf/misc#brame) Started redrawing it, then had the idea to dust off that model from a while back. Ta-da~ The original image is AI-generated and/or crudely photoshopped, but in the process of trying to source it anyway I found multiple incidents of a deer actually getting hit by and flying *into* a bus. [Wild.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FxHEwr-yXDs) No animals were harmed in the making of this image. Realism was mildly harmed, though. I have no process shots! Here are some viewport angles.
Rather than build a to-scale bus with nicely-aligned seats, I set a portion of my viewport to the camera view, scrolled around on the rest, and winged it from there. Had fun futzing around with textures, lighting, and camera settings---all of which tend to be afterthoughts when I spend the bulk of a session on the model itself. Might try it again some time! It's nice to be comfortable enough with Blender to fuck around and find out, without needing a guide every other vertex. Vaguely referenced [this photo of an actual bus](https://www.istockphoto.com/photo/old-bus-interior-gm530800455-54826864), and [Ryan King's tutorial on depth of field](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ahT3P274EMw)). That's all for once!
