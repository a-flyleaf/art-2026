In 2018 I played Doki Doki Literature Club! and got so fixated on the concept of an incomplete-reality!glitched-game!world (with people stuck in it!) that the whole thing turned into the original project 404. It launched briefly as [a web-based ?comic-site-thing](https://a-flyleaf.github.io/hello-world), then petered out when I got stuck on a scene and became dissatisfied with the story in general. Anyway, it sounds like [The Amazing Digital Circus](https://www.glitchprod.com/digital-circus) does everything thematically that I wanted to accomplish with 404 and then some. I haven't watched it yet because I don't like getting into things that are still in progress. The finale premiered on YouTube a few days ago, it continues to sound extremely relevant to my interests, and rather than start watching it I drew this.
time taken: ~3 hours. Background Pomni crucifixion (a delightful string of words, that) from [a neat article about the series](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/interviews/the-amazing-digital-circus-creator-gooseworx-on-developing-the-internets-hottest-animated-pilot-234217.html).
